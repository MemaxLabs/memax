Data retention policy (draft, pending legal review):

- Free tier: memories auto-archived after 90 days of no access, permanently deleted after 180 days
- Pro tier: no auto-archival, user-initiated deletion only
- Team tier: configurable per-hub retention (30/60/90/180/365 days), admin can set minimum retention for compliance
- All tiers: deleted memories are soft-deleted for 30 days (recoverable), then hard-deleted
- Audit logs retained for 2 years regardless of tier
- User data export available on request (GDPR Article 20), response SLA: 72 hours
