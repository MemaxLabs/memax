## Sourdough Bread Recipe — No-Knead Method

A simpler sourdough bread method that doesn't require kneading. Good for weekday baking when time is limited.

**Ingredients:**
- 400g bread flour
- 100g whole wheat flour
- 350ml water (room temp)
- 100g active sourdough starter
- 10g salt

**Method:**
1. Mix flour, water, and starter in a large bowl. Rest 30 minutes (autolyse).
2. Add salt, fold gently to incorporate. Cover and bulk ferment 8-10 hours at room temperature.
3. Shape gently on a floured surface — stretch and fold into a ball, minimal handling.
4. Place in a floured banneton, cover, and cold-proof in the fridge 12-24 hours.
5. Preheat Dutch oven at 250C/480F for 45 minutes.
6. Score the dough, bake covered 25 minutes, then uncovered 20 minutes at 230C/450F.

**Tips:** Higher hydration (75%+) gives a more open crumb but is harder to handle. Start with 70% hydration until comfortable. The long cold proof develops more flavor — 24 hours is better than 12 if you have time.

This is the lazy version of my usual recipe. Less control over fermentation but very forgiving.
