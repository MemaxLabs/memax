My sourdough starter and basic loaf recipe, refined over about 15 bakes since I started in February 2026.

## Starter Maintenance

- **Name:** "Bubbles" (named day 7 when it finally doubled)
- **Flour:** 50/50 mix of King Arthur bread flour and whole wheat
- **Feeding ratio:** 1:5:5 (starter:flour:water by weight)
- **Schedule:** Feed once daily at 8 AM. If baking, feed the night before at 10 PM for a 10 AM bake.
- **Temperature:** Keep on top of the fridge where it stays around 76°F.
- **Peak timing:** Starter doubles in 4-6 hours after feeding. The float test should pass by hour 5.

## Basic Sourdough Loaf

### Ingredients
- 100g active starter (passes float test)
- 375g bread flour (King Arthur)
- 25g whole wheat flour
- 275g water at 85°F
- 9g fine sea salt

### Steps
1. **Autolyse (9:00 AM):** Mix flour and water, let rest 45 minutes.
2. **Add starter and salt (9:45 AM):** Incorporate starter, then salt with a splash of water. Pinch and fold until combined.
3. **Bulk ferment (10:00 AM–2:00 PM):** 4 hours at room temp (~74°F). Do 4 sets of stretch and folds, one every 30 minutes for the first 2 hours.
4. **Pre-shape (2:00 PM):** Gently shape into a round, bench rest 20 minutes.
5. **Final shape (2:20 PM):** Shape into a tight boule, place seam-side up in a floured banneton.
6. **Cold retard:** Cover with a shower cap, refrigerate 12-16 hours.
7. **Bake next morning:** Preheat Dutch oven in oven at 500°F for 1 hour. Score the dough, bake covered 20 minutes, then uncovered at 450°F for 25 minutes.
8. **Cool:** Let cool on a wire rack for at least 1 hour before cutting.

## Troubleshooting Notes
- If the dough is too sticky during shaping, wet your hands rather than adding flour.
- If the ear doesn't open well, try a deeper score at a shallower angle (about 30 degrees).
- Dense crumb usually means underfermented — let bulk go longer or use warmer water.
