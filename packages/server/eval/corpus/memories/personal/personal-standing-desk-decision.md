Decided to switch from my IKEA MALM sitting desk to a Flexispot E7 standing desk after three months of research and worsening lower back pain.

## Why I Switched

I was sitting 10+ hours/day between work and personal coding. Started getting persistent lower back pain in December 2025 that wasn't going away with stretching alone. My physical therapist, Dr. Nguyen, specifically recommended alternating between sitting and standing throughout the day.

## Research Summary

Looked at three options:
1. **Flexispot E7** ($499) — dual motor, 275 lb capacity, programmable height presets. Best value for the build quality.
2. **Uplift V2** ($599) — similar specs, slightly better cable management, but $100 more.
3. **IKEA BEKANT** ($349) — cheapest but single motor, wobbles at standing height per reviews.

Went with the Flexispot E7. The dual motor matters because single-motor desks wobble noticeably at standing height, especially when typing.

## Ergonomic Setup

Based on recommendations from the Wirecutter guide and my PT:
- **Standing height:** Elbows at 90 degrees, monitor at eye level. Set preset 1 to 44 inches.
- **Sitting height:** Same elbow rule. Set preset 2 to 29 inches.
- **Anti-fatigue mat:** Got the Ergodriven Topo ($99), the one with the terrain features. Makes a big difference for foot comfort.
- **Schedule:** 25 minutes standing, 35 minutes sitting. Using the Flexispot reminder app.

## Results After 6 Weeks

- Lower back pain has dropped from a 6/10 to a 2/10
- Energy levels noticeably better in the afternoon slump (2-4 PM)
- Calves were sore the first two weeks but adapted
- Productivity feels the same — no negative impact from switching
