## Q2 Pricing Decision: $9/$19 Tiers

After analyzing competitor pricing (Mem0 free-only, Supermemory $12 flat), usage data from 340 beta users, and willingness-to-pay survey results (n=87), we settled on three paid tiers:

- **Free** -- 50 memories, 1 personal hub, 100 recalls/day
- **Pro ($9/mo)** -- 5,000 memories, 3 hubs, unlimited recalls, config sync
- **Team ($19/seat/mo)** -- shared team hubs, RBAC, audit log, priority support

Key rationale:

1. **$9 anchors below Notion AI ($10) and GitHub Copilot ($10).** Developers compare us to tools in the same budget line. Staying under $10 removes friction in expense-report approvals.
2. **$19 team tier justifies per-seat pricing** because team hubs create network effects -- each additional seat increases shared context value for everyone.
3. **Free tier is generous enough for solo hobbyists** but bumps against the 50-memory ceiling within 2-3 weeks of active agent use, creating natural upgrade pressure.

We rejected a $29 enterprise tier for now. Enterprise deals (Acme Corp, etc.) will be custom-quoted until we have 3+ signed contracts to anchor a self-serve price.
