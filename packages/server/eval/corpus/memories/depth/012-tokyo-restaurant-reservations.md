Restaurant reservations for the Tokyo trip, April 18-25, 2026.

## Confirmed Reservations

### Day 1 (April 19) — Arrival Dinner
**Omoide Yokocho (思い出横丁)**
- No reservation needed (street food alley). Plan to arrive around 7 PM.
- Must try: yakitori at Asadachi, grilled offal at Suzuki.

### Day 2 (April 20) — Shibuya
**Afuri Ramen (阿夫利) — Ebisu location**
- Walk-in only. Best to arrive before 11:30 AM to avoid the lunch rush.
- Get the yuzu shio ramen (signature dish).

### Day 3 (April 21) — Asakusa
**Sometaro (染太郎) — Okonomiyaki**
- Reserved for 2 at 6:30 PM. Confirmation: SOM-2026-0421-7823.
- Historic building, you cook at your own table. Cash only.

### Day 4 (April 22) — Kamakura
**Bowls (ボウルズ) — Kamakura**
- Reserved for 2 at 12:00 PM. Confirmation: BWL-0422-1200.
- Organic donburi near the beach. Recommended by a food blog.

### Day 5 (April 23) — Tsukiji/Ginza
**Sushi Dai (寿司大) — Toyosu Market**
- No reservation. Must arrive by 5:30 AM for the queue. Worth it — omakase is only ¥4,000.
**Ginza Kyubey (銀座久兵衛)**
- Reserved for 2 at 7:00 PM. Confirmation: KYB-0423-1900-EVAL.
- Counter seats. Budget: ¥25,000/person for the omakase course.

### Day 6 (April 24) — Ueno
**Innsyoutei (韻松亭)**
- Reserved for 2 at 12:30 PM. Confirmation: IST-0424-1230.
- Traditional Japanese cuisine inside Ueno Park. Seasonal bento lunch.
