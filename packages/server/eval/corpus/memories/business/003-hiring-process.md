Hiring process for engineering roles:

1. Resume screen (David or hiring manager, 48h SLA)
2. Intro call (30 min, culture + motivation, any team member)
3. Technical assessment (take-home, 4h time-boxed, reviewed blind)
4. Technical deep-dive (60 min, walk through assessment + system design)
5. Team fit (45 min, 2 team members, behavioral questions from the bank)
6. Offer stage (comp band check with Sarah, reference check optional for senior+)

Rejection emails go out within 24h of each stage. Candidates get written feedback after the technical stage regardless of outcome.
