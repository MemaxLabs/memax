## Pricing Analysis — February Draft

Early pricing exploration from February before we had beta usage data. Initial proposal:

- **Free** -- 25 memories, 1 hub, 50 recalls/day
- **Pro ($5/mo)** -- 2,000 memories, 2 hubs, 500 recalls/day
- **Team ($15/seat/mo)** -- shared hubs, basic RBAC, email support

Rationale at the time: Price low to maximize adoption. $5 is impulse-buy territory for developers. Compete on price against Mem0 (free-only) by offering a paid tier with dramatically more storage.

**Why this was rejected:** Sarah's willingness-to-pay survey (n=87) showed developers would pay $9-12 for a Pro tier. The $5 price point left money on the table and signaled "cheap tool" rather than "professional infrastructure." The 25-memory free tier was also too restrictive — users churned before experiencing value.

See the Q2 pricing decision for the final approved tiers ($9/$19).
