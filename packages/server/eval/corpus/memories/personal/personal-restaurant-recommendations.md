Restaurant recommendations from the past few weeks. Sarah Kim and I have been exploring new spots in the Mission and Sunset districts.

## Thai Restaurants

**Kin Khao** (Union Square, 55 Cyril Magnin St)
- Went on March 18 with Sarah. Michelin-starred Thai. The crab curry (Khao Soi with Dungeness crab, $34) was outstanding — rich coconut broth with pickled mustard greens. Sarah's green papaya salad was excellent too.
- Reservations recommended, especially weekends. We waited 20 minutes even with a reservation.
- Rating: 9/10. Worth the price.

**Farmhouse Kitchen Thai** (710 Florida St, Mission)
- Tried on March 15 for lunch. More casual than Kin Khao. The boat noodles ($18) and crispy pork belly pad kra pao ($22) were great. Generous portions.
- No reservation needed for lunch. Dinner gets busy.
- Rating: 7.5/10. Good everyday Thai spot.

## Japanese Restaurants

**Ju-Ni** (1335 Fulton St, NoPa)
- Omakase only, 18-course sushi for $198/person. Went March 20 for Sarah's birthday dinner. Chef Geoffrey Lee sources fish directly from Tokyo's Toyosu Market. The otoro and uni were the best I've had in SF.
- Must book 2-3 weeks in advance. Only 12 seats at the counter.
- Rating: 9.5/10. Special occasion place.

**Marufuku Ramen** (1581 Webster St, Japantown)
- Quick solo lunch on March 12. Hakata-style tonkotsu ramen ($17). Rich, creamy pork broth with thin noodles. Got the extra chashu topping ($4). Always consistent.
- Usually a 15-30 minute wait at peak lunch. Go at 11:30 AM to avoid the line.
- Rating: 8/10. Best ramen in SF.
